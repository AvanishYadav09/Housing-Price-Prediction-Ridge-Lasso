Econometric Methodology & Regularization Framework

A comprehensive technical review of Ordinary Least Squares, Ridge, and Lasso regression under structural multicollinearity. Exploring mathematical loss formulations, Variance Inflation Factor (VIF), and soft-thresholding coordinate descent.

1. The Multicollinearity Phenomenon in Housing Data
Ill-Conditioned Gram Matrix

In real estate valuation, architectural dimensions frequently exhibit near-perfect linear dependencies. In our dataset:

  • total_interior_sqft ≈ living_area_sqft + ε(0, 100)
  • footprint_sqft ≈ 0.75 × living_area_sqft + ε(0, 80)

When predictors are collinear, the columns of design matrix X become linearly dependent. As a result, the Gram matrix (XTX) approaches singularity (determinant → 0), causing the inverted matrix (XTX)-1 to have explosive diagonal elements. This inflates the variance of coefficient estimates, making weights unreliable and sensitive to slight data perturbations.

2. Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) Diagnostic
VIF > 10 = Severe

VIF measures how much the variance of an estimated regression coefficient is inflated due to collinearity with other features. For each predictor xj:

VIFj = 1 / (1 - Rj2)

Where Rj2 is the coefficient of determination obtained from regressing xj onto all remaining p - 1 predictors.

3. Objective Loss Functions & Optimization
Comparative Mathematics

A. Ordinary Least Squares (Linear Regression)

minβ { Σi=1n (yi - xiTβ)2 } = minβ || y - Xβ ||22

Analytical Solution: β̂ = (XTX)-1 XTy.
Pathology: In our housing data, OLS assigns footprint a coefficient of -3,905.10. Larger houses do not lower market value; this negative sign is a mathematical artifact of matrix inversion on correlated columns.

B. Ridge Regression (Tikhonov / L2 Regularization)

minβ { || y - Xβ ||22 + α Σj=1p βj2 } = minβ { || y - Xβ ||22 + α || β ||22 }

Analytical Solution: β̂Ridge = (XTX + α I)-1 XTy (where the intercept row/col in I is not penalized).
Effect: Adding α along the diagonal guarantees that (XTX + αI) is non-singular and strictly positive-definite. Ridge smoothly shrinks weights and restores footprint to a positive +24,935.63.

C. Lasso Regression (L1 Regularization & Feature Selection)

minβ { 1/(2n) || y - Xβ ||22 + α Σj=1p |βj| }

Because the L1 norm |βj| has a sharp vertex at βj = 0, contour intersections occur on the coordinate axes, setting redundant coefficients identically to zero.

Coordinate Descent Update: For each feature j, calculating partial residual correlation ρj:

βj = S(ρj, α) = sign(ρj) × max(0, |ρj| - α)

Result: Lasso forces footprint_sqft coefficient to 0.0000, automatically discovering that footprint adds no incremental information beyond living area.

4. Feature Standardization & Data Leakage Prevention
StandardScaler Protocol

Because regularization penalties (Σβ² and Σ|β|) penalize absolute coefficient magnitude, features with larger units (such as lot size in thousands of sq. ft.) would be unfairly penalized compared to car counts (0-3).

zij = (xij - μj,train) / σj,train

Strict Data Leakage Rule: Means (μ) and standard deviations (σ) are computed exclusively on the 80% training partition (N = 1,600). The 20% test partition (N = 400) and user prediction queries are transformed using the saved training parameters without re-estimation.
