Linear Regression (OLS)
Unpenalized

Minimizes standard sum of squared residuals without constraint. Under severe multicollinearity, the feature matrix becomes ill-conditioned, yielding erratic and counter-intuitive negative weights.

Ridge Regression
L2 Regularization (α=10)

Applies an L2 squared weight penalty (λ Σβ²). Adds variance stabilization to (X′X + αI), distributing weights proportionally across correlated features rather than dropping them.

Dataset & Controlled Econometric Formulation

The underlying dataset contains 2,000 residential property records generated with deliberate collinearity between living_area_sqft, total_interior_sqft, and footprint_sqft. The target variable sale_price was modeled using the ground-truth equation:

sale_price = 50,000 + 150 × living_area + 10 × lot_size + 15,000 × garage + 8,000 × rooms + ε(0, 25,000)

Notice that total_interior_sqft and footprint_sqft have a true economic coefficient of zero. Lasso successfully identifies this ground-truth sparsity by eliminating footprint area.
